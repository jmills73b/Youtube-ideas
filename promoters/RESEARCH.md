# The Promoters: research notes

*The Promoters* is a parody. The characters are cartoon caricatures, the voices are generic synthetic voices rather than imitations, and most of the dialogue is invented. This file records the real public dispute the sketch is built on. It also marks which lines echo things that were actually reported (the `# REAL` lines in `script.py`).

The fight contract has **not** been published, so nothing here claims what it actually says. Only what each side said in public is reported.

## The real story, September–October 2026

Everything else is invented for comedy, including:

- the binder, page 40 and August;
- the whiteboard;
- the name badge;
- the raised hand;
- the blue folder;
- Gary the tea man;
- the empty meeting room.

## Style

The sketch borrows the general conventions of the British workplace mockumentary:

- handheld documentary camera;
- talking heads to camera with lower-third captions;
- awkward silences, with crash zooms on looks to camera;
- a drab open-plan office.

It uses no footage, music, characters or dialogue from any existing show. The music is a short original guitar figure synthesised in code.
